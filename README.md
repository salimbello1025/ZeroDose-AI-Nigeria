# ZeroDose AI Nigeria

An AI-assisted tracker that helps primary health centres find and follow up children who missed routine vaccines.

## What it does
- Ranks overdue children by days overdue, distance and previous missed visits
- Sends reminders to mothers in Hausa, English and Pidgin
- Gives health workers a daily outreach list: who to visit, and in what order

## Safety
AI ranks and reminds only. It never advises on vaccines or medicines. The nurse always decides.

## Privacy
No real child records, names or phone numbers are stored in this repository. All test data is fake or coded.

## Status
Pilot planned at one primary health centre in Kano State. Prototype in progress.

## Roadmap
After the Kano pilot, other states and more Nigerian languages. This is a roadmap, not a promise.
